# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two overlapping audiences. Primary: people reviewing the developer's **portfolio** (recruiters, clients, peers) judging frontend and motion craft in the first few seconds. Secondary, in-fiction: hungry visitors to **SLICE**, a wood-fired Neapolitan pizzeria, deciding to order. Context is a quick, curious first visit on desktop or phone; the job is "show me something impressive / make me want a slice."

## Product Purpose

A single-page landing showpiece that recreates the look, transitions and "wow" of cravburgers.shop, reinterpreted for pizza. It exists to demonstrate advanced, tasteful motion (ingredient-building loader, color-band reveal, scroll choreography, a scroll-scrubbed airplane route) and original SVG illustration, all in a cohesive bold identity. Success = a visitor asks "how was this built?" and remembers it. Fully Polish.

## Brand Personality

Bold, playful, appetizing, confident. Retro-cartoon / Y2K mascot energy: googly eyes, white-gloved hands, puffy outlined poster type, sticker badges. Loud on purpose. Voice is warm and direct, never corporate.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS-cream minimalism. Flat corporate landing pages. Editorial-magazine restraint (display-serif + italic + ruled columns). Timid palettes and safe centered-hero layouts. Anything that reads "AI-template."

## Design Principles

- **Wow on first load.** The loader and reveal are the handshake; they must earn the visit.
- **Personality over restraint.** Maximalism is the brief; commit to the cartoon language end to end.
- **Faithful homage, original execution.** Match the reference's interaction model; draw the art and write the code from scratch.
- **Fast despite loud.** Heavy visuals, but transform/opacity motion, lazy media, reduced-motion fallback.
- **Appetite is the metric.** Real, decisive food photography over decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. `prefers-reduced-motion` fully honored (loader/reveal/scroll motion collapse to instant). Keyboard-operable nav and menu, visible focus ring. Polish-language content with full latin-ext glyph coverage in all fonts.
